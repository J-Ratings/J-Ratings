About J-Ratings

J-Ratings is a sports ratings site built around Elo. It shows how strong teams and players are, how that strength changes over time, and the results behind the ratings.

How Elo works

Elo moves rating points between competitors after each result. Beat someone stronger and you gain more. Lose to someone weaker and you lose more. Expected results cause smaller changes.

The rating gap also gives an expected score:

Expected score = 1 / (1 + 10((Opponent rating − Your rating) / 400))

A rating difference of 0 means an even contest. Positive numbers mean the first competitor is rated higher; negative numbers mean they are rated lower.

In sports where draws are possible, expected score is not exactly the same as win probability. A win counts as 1, a draw as 0.5 and a loss as 0.

What can you do here?

  • See current ratings and rankings.
  • Look back through rating history.
  • View past games and results.
  • Compare peaks and different eras.
  • Explore several sports using the same rating-based approach.
  • Use tournament and simulation tools as they are added.

Why J-Ratings?

Ratings are more useful when you can see the story behind them. J-Ratings combines the number itself with the games, history and context that produced it.
