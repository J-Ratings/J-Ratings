Actual Premier League results are used up to the selected date. Each club's J-Ratings Elo at that date is loaded from its rating history, so information from other competitions is retained. The remaining league fixtures are then simulated with Elo updating after every simulated match. Equal-points ties are currently broken randomly because exact future scorelines are not yet simulated.
