The 2026–27 Coupe de France is the 110th season of the main football cup competition of France. The competition was organised by the French Football Federation (FFF) and is open to all clubs in French football, as well as clubs from the overseas departments and territories (Guadeloupe, French Guiana, Martinique, Mayotte, Tahiti, Réunion, Saint Martin, and Saint Pierre and Miquelon).

The defending champions are RC Lens, who defeated OGC Nice 3–1 in the previous year's final for their maiden Coupe de France title.

Dates

[edit]

Dates for the first and second rounds are set by regional leagues, while the FFF sets the calendar for all matches in subsequent rounds.[1] From the third round to the round of 32, all matches take place on weekends. Afterwards, each match takes place on Wednesday evening.

All clubs from the amateur departmental leagues (tiers 9-15) enter at round 1, and all clubs from the amateur regional leagues enter in round 2 (tiers 5-7). This is not a hard rule, and exceptions are frequently made based on the number of clubs in a given district.

Round 7

[edit]

The qualified teams were divided into nine distinct groups of equal level and geography to avoid long travel. The 155 clubs that qualified from the sixth round are joined by all 18 clubs from Ligue 2, as well as the regional representatives from Mayotte, New Caledonia, and Tahiti.[2]
