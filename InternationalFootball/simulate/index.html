Knockout stage

Actual competition results before the selected date are used. Each club's J-Ratings Elo at that date is loaded from its rating history unless the user overrides it. Remaining league fixtures are then simulated with Elo updating after every match. Forced results are applied exactly. If home advantage is enabled, the selected Elo bonus affects match probability only. Equal-points ties are currently broken randomly because exact future scorelines are not yet simulated.
