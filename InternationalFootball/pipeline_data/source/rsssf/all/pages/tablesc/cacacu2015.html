Notes:
All teams Tuzos: Club de Fútbol Pachuca’s subsidiares.
Atlas D.F: Atlas Fútbol Club’s subsidiare.
Chivas Kabah: Club Deportivo Guadalajara’s subsidiare.
Universidad de Chile México: Club Universidad de Chile’s subsidiare in Mexico.

Club Social y Deportivo Yupanqui was authorized by Argentine Football Association to play the U-18 cup.
Club Atlético Social y Deportivo Camioneros was allied of Yupanqui during 2015.
Camioneros played instead of Yupanqui in Male U-18 cup.
