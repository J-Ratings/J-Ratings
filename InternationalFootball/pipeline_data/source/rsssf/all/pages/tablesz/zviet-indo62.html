South Vietnam national team tour of Indonesia 1962

Results

Note: matches were 80 minutes in duration.

14-Feb-62  Persidja  3-4  South Vietnam     [HT 1-2]
  [Pham Huynh Tam Lang 22' OG, Sutjipto Suntoro 42' 76' / Nguyen Van Quang 28', Trinh Nguu 35', Do Thoi Vinh 50', Huynh Van Thuyen 74']
  [Stadion Ikada, Djakarta; att: 17000]
  [Persidja: Adenan; Nany Zailani, Sarwonol Madnoer, Ismono, Suranto; Sudibjo, Siang Soej, Sutjipto, Suwardjo, Wagiman.]
  [S.Vietnam: Tran Van Duc; Tran Chieu Khoa, Phan Duong Cam; Nguyen Ngoc Thanh, Pham Huynh Tam Lang, Quach Hoi;
              Tran Van Nhung, Do Thoi Vinh, Nguyen Van Quang, Huynh Van Thuyen, Trinh Nguu.]

17-Feb-62  Persebaja-Persema kombinasi  0-2  South Vietnam     [HT 0-1]
  [Trinh Nguu 10', Nguyen Van Quang 64']
  [Stadion Tambaksari, Surabaja]

19-Feb-62  P.S.S.I. Kombinasi*  1-2   South Vietnam     [HT 1-1]
  [Fattah Hidajat 37' / Tran Ta 20', Tran Van Nhung 55']
  [Stadion Ikada, Djakarta; att: 45000]
  [P.S.S.I. Kombinasi: Tjong; Ishak Udin, Matseh; Sian Liong, Tan Liong Houw (Muchadi 41'), Ipong;
            Mursabdo, Agam (Frans Jo 41'), Sonny Sandra, Fattah Hidajat (Suhendar 41'), Eddy Simon.]
  [Referee: Kuntadi (Solo, Indonesia).]

* P.S.S.I. Kombinasi was a combined team selected by the Indonesian national football association,
  but it was not considered as the full national team.

Note: a Reuter report dated 24th February 1962 said that Indonesian police had arrested 16 Indonesian players,
      two referees and 16 Chinese businessmen following allegations that the match on 19th February was fixed.

Notes: the national team was often referred to in the local media as the "Vietnam All-Stars" or as the
       "Saigon All-Stars" (even when including players from outwith Saigon).  The South Vietnamese
       association decided to include some newer players for this tour, to revitalise thenational team
       for future tournaments.  This led to foreign media referring to the team as a weakened selection
       (i.e. a B-team or a youth team) but this was not the case.  Many of the players involved were
       regulars in major international tournaments.  The team returned to Saigon on 20th February.
