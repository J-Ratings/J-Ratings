Tiger Cup and AFF Cup

Tournament for full national teams from South East Asia, held biannually. The first five editions were known as Tiger Cup. Renamed AFF Cup in 2007 after end of Tiger Beer sponsorship. From 2008, Suzuki bought the rights for the competition. Alternates with the South East Asian Games, currently an age-restricted (U-23) tournament.
