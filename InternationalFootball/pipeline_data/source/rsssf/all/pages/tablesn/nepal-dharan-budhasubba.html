Budha Subba Gold Cup (Dharan)

Annual international tournament organised by Dharan FC. Rules on duration of matches and tie-breakers changed several times; in particular in the earlier seasons, matches were played over 2x40 minutes (but in most later tournaments 2x45 minutes were played), while draws in regulation time were often followed immediately by a penalty shoot-out, but in other editions extra time (either of 15, 20 or 30 minutes) was played.
Between 2012 and 2017, during the reconstruction of Dharan Stadium (Dharan Rangasala), the tournament was instead staged at the ANFA Technical Centre in Dharan.
