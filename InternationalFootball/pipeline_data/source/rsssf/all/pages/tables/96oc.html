Results

Solomon Islands qualified as winners of Melanesia Cup 1994
Tahiti qualified as winners of Polynesia Cup 1994

First Round
(home and away)
 
10-11-1995 New Zealand     0-0 Australia
15-11-1995 Australia       3-0 New Zealand              
[NB: also counted for the Trans-Tasman Cup.]

17-11-1995 Solomon Islands 0-1 Tahiti 
11- 5-1995 Tahiti          2-1 Solomon Islands  
 
Final
(home and away)

26-10-1996 Tahiti          0-6 Australia
 1-11-1996 Australia       5-0 Tahiti
    
Australia won the right to represent OFC at the Intercontinental Cup.
