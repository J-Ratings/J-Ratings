Results

All the OFC member nations were ranked from 1 to 11 based on October 
2001 FIFA rankings; New Caledonia (not a FIFA member) was ranked in 
position 12.

Teams ranked from 7 to 12 contested a round robin qualifying tournament. 
The winner and the runner-up advanced to the Final Tournament, held 
in Auckland, New Zealand.

Final Tournament 
(Auckland, New Zealand - July 5 to 15, 2002)

Eight teams participated: teams ranked in Oceania from 1 to 6, based on October
2001 FIFA rankings; and the winners and runners-up from the qualifying tournament.

Two groups of four teams, with Australia and New Zealand as seeded teams. 

Participating teams: (1) Australia 
                     (2) New Zealand
                     (3) Fiji
                     (4) Tahiti
                     (5) Solomon Islands
                     (6) Vanuatu
                     (7) Papua New Guinea
                     (8) New Caledonia
