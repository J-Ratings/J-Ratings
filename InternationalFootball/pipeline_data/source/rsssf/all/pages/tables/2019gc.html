CONCACAF Championship, Gold Cup 2019

Caribbean Zone: Trinidad & Tobago, Haiti, Martinique, Curaçao, Bermuda, Cuba,
                Jamaica and Guyana.
Central American Zone: Costa Rica, Panama, Honduras, Nicaragua and El Salvador.
North American Zone: Canada, Mexico and USA.

NB: the qualifying stage of the 2018-2020 Nations League served as qualifying
    tournament.
