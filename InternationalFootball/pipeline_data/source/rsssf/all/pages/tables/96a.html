Final Tournament (in South Africa)

 


NB: South Africa replaced Kenya as hosts.

Nigeria withdrew before the final tournament on the grounds of
insufficient safety guarantees - politics (the recent hangings
of human rights activitists and the international reaction on
that, also from South Africa) probably played a major role.
Guinea was asked by the CAF to replace them in Group 3 (as best 
losers in qualifying), but the country refused the invitation, 
citing lack of preparation time.
