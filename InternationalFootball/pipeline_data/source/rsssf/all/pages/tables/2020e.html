UEFA Nations League Playoffs

 

NB: in each of the four "Leagues" of the UEFA Nations League 2018/19, one place at
    the final tournament is contested between the four group winners;
    if group winners have already qualified directly, their place is taken by the
    next highest-ranked country in their category;
    if a "League" has fewer than 4 entrants (because the other members qualified
    directly), its remaining slots are allocated to the best teams from the next
    lower "League" according to the overall rankings; because of this, three slots
    from League A were "inherited" by League B; as only four countries from League
    B failed to qualify directly, all three superflous spots from League A were
    eventually taken by sides from League C: Iceland were drawn together with
    Bulgaria, Hungary and Romania, three of the four teams from League C who failed
    to win their Nations League group.

Final Tournament

 

NB: postponed to 2021 due to Covid-19 pandemic;
    before the final tournament proper in London, an absurd "Phase Platini" was played
    all over Europe, consisting of a group stage and two knock-out rounds, in which six
    countries played all their group matches at home (Denmark, England, Germany, Italy,
    the Netherlands and Spain) while other teams (such as Belgium and Switzerland) were
    subjected to arduous travel trajectories reminiscent of the odyssee Platini's France 
    was to endure at the 1950 World Cup, from which they then withdrew out of protest;
    unsurprisingly, all four semifinalists belonged to the "selected six".
