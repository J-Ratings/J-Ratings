African Nations Cup 1959

Qualifying Tournament

None was necessary.  Only three countries entered.

Final Tournament (Cairo, Egypt)

Note: Egypt, together with Syria, was part of the United Arab Republic (U.A.R.) in this period.
      However the team itself was selected from only Egyptian players.

All matches were played at Al-Ahly Stadium in Cairo.
