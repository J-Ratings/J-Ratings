Argentina - Copa de Oro (Gold Cup) - 1936

 The cup was played between CA San Lorenzo de Almagro, Copa de Honor champion and CA River Plate, Championship winner.
 It was recognized as a First Division Championship by the AFA, following a letter signed by its General Secretary on July 4, 2013.

Sunday, December 20, 1936.

CA River Plate 4:2 CA San Lorenzo de Almagro, field: CA Independiente, city: Avellaneda.


CA River Plate: Sebastián Santos Sirni; Luis Vasini, Alberto Cuello; Esteban Malazzo, José María Minella (captain), Aarón Wergifker;
                Carlos Desiderio Peucelle, Renato Cesarini, Bernabé Ferreyra, José Manuel Moreno, Adolfo Alfredo Pedernera.
                Coach: Emérico Hirschl.

CA San Lorenzo de Almagro: Sebastián Inocencio Gualco; Oscar Tarrío (captain), Lorenzo A. Gilli; Ismael Salvador Arrese, 
                           Mario Rosario Scavone, Alberto Chividini; Rubén Obdulio Cavadini, Ricardo Alarcón, Genaro Canteli, 
                           Diego García, Miguel Ángel Pantó.
                           Coach: José Hipólito Fossa.

Referee: José Bartolomé Macías. Linesmen: Eduardo Forte and Domingo Solari.

Goals:
      1-0 39m Renato Cesarini.
      1-1 55m Miguel Pantó.
      2-1 73m Bernabé Ferreyra (RP) shot, it hit Rubén Chividini (SLA) and goal.
      3-1 76m Adolfo Pedernera.
      3-2 81m Rubén Cavadini (other sources Genaro Canteli).
      4-2 86m Renato Cesarini.

Sent off: 64m Emérico Hirschl.
