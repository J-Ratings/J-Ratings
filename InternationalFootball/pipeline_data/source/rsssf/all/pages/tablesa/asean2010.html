AFF Suzuki Cup 2010

AFF = ASEAN Football Federation

Qualification

Indonesia, Malaysia, Myanmar, Singapore, Thailand, and
Vietnam qualified automatically.

The other countries played a qualification tournament in 
Vientiane, from which Laos and the Philippines qualified.
