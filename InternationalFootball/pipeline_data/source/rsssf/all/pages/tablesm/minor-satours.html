South-American National Teams Tournaments

Apart from the Copa America, many friendly minor tournaments ("Copas") have been organized over the years between national sides in South America.

Almost all of these tournaments have been played on an irregular basis and involved only two teams. Some tournaments only had one edition and almost all were played before the seventies. This page will contain links to all the tournaments below. Only the tournaments in italics had more than one edition.
